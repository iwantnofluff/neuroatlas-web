1. What NeuroAtlas is 2. Tech stack 3. Pages built 4. Key systems

1What NeuroAtlas is

NeuroAtlas is a screenless stress-management wearable — the NA·01 band — that reads physiological signal (HRV, breathing, stress load) and surfaces it through a companion app. The website is the pre-launch marketing site: it introduces the product, explains how it works, and captures leads for an India-focused pilot program ahead of a real purchase flow.

3Pages built

/ — Homepage

Full-screen video hero, real product footage. Scroll-driven sections: the "Method" cards, a bento grid of secondary signals ("Beyond Heart Rate"), a 3D band reveal ("Built To Read You") with the real GLTF model, and a spotlight photo section.

/band — The NA·01 Band

Dedicated product page: a scroll-driven 3D hero showcase of the band model, an editorial "what it doesn't do" section, a design/build section, a Signal vs. Noise comparison sequence, an interactive 3D "Specs" section (click-through reticles reveal sensors/battery/connectivity/ dimensions/compatibility with the model rotating to match), and a closing curtain-reveal CTA.

/the-science — The Science

A dedicated 3D "living signal" hero, an HRV explainer section, a section on the limits of consumer wearables, the neuroscience research basis, and a closing section.

/how-it-works

Hero plus a feature-split walkthrough of the read → interpret → act loop.

/inside-the-app

/for-organisations

4Key systems

3D band model

A real CAD-derived GLTF model of the NA·01 renders across three scenes (homepage reveal, the /band hero showcase, and the /band interactive Specs section), sharing one component (Band.tsx) and one lighting/environment rig. Every part — housing, button, electrode plates, optical window, charging pads — has its own physically-based material tuned to the real product spec (anodised navy aluminium, cobalt button, polished steel, gold contacts), lit by a procedural studio environment (no external HDRI dependency), with a grounding contact shadow under the module.

Global film-grain texture

A subtle, animated noise overlay across every page, tuned to read as cinematic texture over the site's dark navy sections while staying invisible over its light cream sections — a single lightweight SVG asset, no per-page logic.

Waitlist capture

/api/waitlist, isolated from the general newsletter signup so pilot leads and general subscribers don't mix once real persistence is added.

Content (Sanity CMS)

The Journal blog's articles are authored and edited in Sanity Studio at /studio, with live preview wired through Next.js Draft Mode.
